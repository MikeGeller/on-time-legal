On Time — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

On Time is designed to help you plan your morning activities and figure out when you need to wake up.

We believe your personal routines should stay personal. That's why On Time is designed to work offline, without requiring an account or sending your plans to our servers.

Information stored on your device

When you use On Time, the app saves your activity timings, preferences, saved routines, customizations, planned activities, departure times, and preparation buffers.

This information is stored locally so your settings and plans are available when you reopen the app.

We do not operate a server that receives or stores this information.

Information we collect

On Time does not require registration or ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.

We do not intentionally collect personal information or usage analytics through the app, use advertising trackers, or sell your information.

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to provide, including your email address and message. We use it to respond to your inquiry and manage related correspondence.

Third-party services

On Time uses software components needed to operate on Android and iOS.

Apple, Google, and your device's operating system may independently process information related to app downloads, purchases, or device diagnostics according to their own privacy policies.

On Time does not currently provide cloud synchronization or connect your personal plans to external accounts.

Data retention and deletion

Your saved routines, activity timings, and preferences remain on your device until you change them or uninstall the app.

Your current Tomorrow plan is managed according to the app's daily planning cycle and automatically resets at 3 a.m. local time.

Uninstalling On Time generally removes its locally stored application data. Copies may remain in device backups managed by your operating system or backup provider.

Because we do not receive your locally stored plans, we cannot access, retrieve, or delete them remotely.

If you have contacted us by email, you may request deletion of that correspondence by contacting us.

Children's privacy

On Time does not require users to provide personally identifying information and does not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when On Time's features or data practices change.

Updates will be reflected in the published policy, including an updated revision date.
